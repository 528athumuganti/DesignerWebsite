Piero Fornasetti's early life

  • Born on November 10, 1913, in Milan, Italy, into a wealthy middle-class family
  • Showed a passionate drive for visual art from a very young age, which frequently put him at odds with his father, who wanted him to pursue a traditional business career.
  • Enrolled at the prestigious Brera Academy of Fine Arts in Milan to study drawing, but was expelled after just two years for insubordination and defiance toward the rigid academic curriculum
  • After leaving school, he turned to independent self-study, immersing himself in engraving, printing, and classical Italian history
  • In the mid-1930s, he established his own printshop where he experimented with printing graphic designs onto diverse materials like silk scarves, ceramics, and books
  • His turning point came when legendary Italian architect and designer Gio Ponti discovered Fornasetti's printed scarves at the Triennale di Milano. This spark launched one of the most prolific collaborations in 20th-century Italian design
