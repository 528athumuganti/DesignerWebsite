Design Decisions:
The typography within the website gives off a strong presence with it's prominant features within the custom illustrations inside the full-screen background image layers across all pages. The hand-drawn aesthetic brings a warm texture and strong personal artistic identity to the background. It breaks away from rigid digital text, giving the viewer an immediate feel for Piero Fornasetti's signature graphic style and craft

With the light and dark values, it is applied across the multitude of features (such as the altered artworks of Piero Fornasetti) in the background. High contrast between the brightly illuminated centers of the artwork fading into deep edge shadows creates visual weight. It immediately draws the viewer's eye to the critical point of the artwork before guiding their attention toward the downward text.

The color, or the monochromatic palette clearly displayed and carried throughout the entire site, including the black body background, white typography, and styled imagery with tones of grey. The high-contrast black-and-white scheme evokes a classic, dramatic visual illusion. It removes color distractions, allowing the viewer to focus entirely on the line work, textual content, and surrealist visualization. 

Reguarding the line element, it emphasizes the page you are currently on. An animated horizontal line sweeps underneath whichever page link is currently active. This dynamic indicator immediately clarifies the viewer's current location within the site passages and adds modern feedback.

Pattern, rhythm, and emphasis is stationed all throughout the site and is seen in a few backgrounds. The structured grid overlay of the background artwork (such as the repeating face and butterflies). Repeating a singular subject in a grid format transforms standalone artwork into a cohesive, rhythmic background texture. This establishes visual harmony across different pages without competing with the main content.

Lastly, depth is seen everywhere. Using javascript, I made a fade animations that let the backgorund fade away as you scroll down. As the user scrolls down to read the content, the crisp background image smoothly fades out into solid black. This creates a spatial transition that pushes the background into the distance, bringing the internal text box forward for maximum readability.
